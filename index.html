Fight Club , Movie Poster Design

Tone & Texture Study
✦ Designer's POV

Combined high-contrast black-and-white portraits, distressed textures, and fragmented editorial elements to reflect Fight Club’s psychological tension. The layered composition and bold typography create visual conflict, reinforcing the film’s themes of identity, control, and inner rebellion.

Branding
— 03 / Branding

Nutrivaan , Premium Dry Fruits

Luxury Lifestyle Dry Fruits Identity
✦ Designer's POV

This packaging and brand staging focuses on organic elegance. The logo mark elegantly integrates leaf motifs into a classic serif 'N'. The layout relies on rich, golden hour lighting and deep greens to convey purity and premium quality, letting the natural textures of the walnuts and almonds take center stage in the visual hierarchy.

Magazines
— 04 / Magazines

ATELIER , Fashion Magazine Cover

Contemporary Fashion & Culture Publication
✦ Designer's POV

A masterclass in editorial color blocking. The stark crimson red of the model's outfit perfectly aligns with the oversized masthead, creating a bold visual anchor against the muted grey background. The typographic hierarchy uses a highly elegant didone serif, with asymmetrical left-aligned supporting text to balance the central portrait.

Magazine Covers
— 05 / Magazine Covers

HEAVEN , Streetwear Issue

Urban Fashion Editorial
✦ Designer's POV

This cover blends high-contrast photography with a bold, sky-textured display font. The visual hierarchy places the masthead 'HEAVEN' behind the subject to create depth, while the vibrant blue sky contrasts with the monochromatic, textured streetwear subject. The layout conveys a surreal, quick-paced fashion aesthetic.

Based in Delhi, India, working with clients and agencies globally. My approach merges classical Swiss typographic restraint with high-impact digital composition.
